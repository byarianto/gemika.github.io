# Privacy Policy — Prospek CRM for LinkedIn

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-13

**Prospek CRM for LinkedIn** ("the Extension") is a 100% standalone, client-side productivity and personal CRM Chrome extension. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your data under your direct control.

## 1. Data We Collect and Process
When you explicitly interact with the Extension on supported pages (LinkedIn profiles, LinkedIn search results, or Google search results), the Extension may read publicly visible page information you are viewing, such as:
- Prospect name, headline, company, location, connection degree, avatar URL, and profile URL.
- Public contact details (email address or phone number) when you click the **Kontak** button.
- User-configured settings: message templates, sender name/role (`myName`, `myRole`), custom XPath selectors, and your optional Google Gemini API key.

## 2. How Data Is Stored
- **100% Local Browser Storage:** All saved leads, tags, templates, custom XPaths, and preferences are stored locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **No Developer Backend:** The Extension does not operate any remote backend server and does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your leads or browsing history with the developer or third-party data brokers.

## 3. Third-Party Services (User-Initiated Only)
- **Google Gemini API (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`):** Only used if you voluntarily enter your own Gemini API Key in Settings and click **Buat dengan AI**. Only the selected prospect context and your prompt are sent directly from your browser to Google's API under Google's privacy terms.
- **Gmail / Outlook Web Compose (`mail.google.com`, `outlook.live.com`, `outlook.office.com`):** Only accessed when you click **Kirim Email** to pre-fill the recipient, subject, message body, and optional file attachment in your own browser email session.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete individual leads, reset custom XPaths, export your leads to CSV, or uninstall the Extension to permanently remove all data stored in `chrome.storage.local`.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the extension publisher via the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
